Our game is a modern JavaFX implementation of Quarto, a strategic board game where players aim to align four pieces sharing a common attribute — shape, color, size, or fill. Unlike traditional games, in Quarto your opponent chooses your piece, adding a unique twist of strategy and prediction. With a sleek JavaFX interface, dynamic animations, and PostgreSQL-backed statistics tracking, our version brings this classic logic challenge to life in an engaging digital format.
